    How FinMan works

    Read-only access to your books, exact calculations and plain-language advice.

    1

    Connect

    Sign in to QuickBooks Online with read-only access. FinMan can't move money or change your books.

    2

    Calculate

    Every night FinMan syncs your sales, expenses, invoices and bills and recalculates your indicators and forecasts with transparent formulas.

    3

    Explain

    The AI turns those numbers into a short summary and a ranked action list, each with its dollar impact.

    4

    Alert

    You get a weekly brief and instant alerts on the FinMan app (iOS and Android) or by email, for example when cash is about to dip or an invoice goes 30 days late.

    Built for

    • Owner-run businesses with roughly $250K–$5M in yearly revenue
    • Restaurants, clinics, retail, trades and professional services
    • Canadian businesses: CAD, GST/HST and CRA remittance dates built in
    • Bookkeepers who want one view across all their clients

    Your data, protected

    • Read-only QuickBooks connection, which you can revoke any time
    • Encrypted in transit and at rest
    • Never sold, and never used to train AI models
    • Export everything to Excel or Power BI
